About Our Academy

Welcome to Little Orbit Preschool

At Little Orbit Preschool, we believe that every child is a unique star destined to shine. Our mission is to provide a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where young minds can explore, learn, and grow at their own pace. We provide high-quality early childhood education and loving guidance to give your child the perfect launchpad for life.

Our Philosophy

A Crucial Journey of Discovery & Lifelong Learning

We view early childhood as a crucial journey of discovery. Our curriculum is designed to spark curiosity, foster creativity, and build foundational learning skills. By balancing structured play with interactive learning, we ensure that children develop a lifelong love for knowledge while building vital social and emotional skills.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Little Orbit Preschool admissions, daily routines, safety, and our child-centered approach.

We welcome children from ages 1.5 years to 6 years across four developmental stages:

  • Playgroup: 1.5 to 2.5 years (Sensory exploration & motor skills)
  • Nursery: 2.5 to 3.5 years (Language, social habits & curiosity)
  • Junior KG: 3.5 to 4.5 years (Early phonics, numeracy & creativity)
  • Senior KG: 4.5 to 5.5+ years (School readiness, science & reading foundation)

To ensure attentive, loving, and personalized care, we maintain a low ratio of 1:8 in Playgroup & Nursery and 1:12 in Kindergarten. Each classroom is staffed with a certified lead educator and a caring support assistant.

Child safety is our highest priority at Little Orbit:

  • 100% CCTV-monitored classrooms, indoor play areas, and campus perimeters.
  • Child-safe non-toxic materials, rounded furniture corners, and soft play flooring.
  • Daily sanitization cycles for toys, play areas, and child-friendly washrooms.
  • Strict entry verification and designated parent pickup authorization protocols.

We believe education is a partnership with parents. We provide regular updates via daily digital diary notes, photo highlights of milestone activities, monthly one-on-one parent-teacher meetings, and special open house sessions throughout the academic year.

You can schedule a friendly campus tour directly using the "Book a Campus Tour" button on this website, or call us at +91 93560 62067. During your visit, you will tour our classrooms, meet our educators, and receive our 2026–2027 admissions welcome kit.
