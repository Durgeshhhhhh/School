PUT YOUR SCHOOL EVENT PHOTOS HERE!
====================================

You can save your photos in this folder with convenient names like:
- annual-day-1.jpg
- sports-day-1.jpg
- festival-celebration.jpg
- daycare-play.jpg

Then, in your "index.html" file, link to them like:
<img src="images/events/annual-day-1.jpg" alt="Annual Day Dance">

See the main README.md in the root folder for full instructions.
